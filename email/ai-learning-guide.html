Three quick AI wins for your practice
Hi,
Here are three things you can do this week, with tools you already have. None of them need a new subscription, and none of them hand your voice over to a machine.
1
Session notes into a follow-up email
Record a 60-second voice memo after each session. Let an assistant turn it into a warm follow-up in your own voice. You read it, fix one line, send. Ten minutes a day back.
2
One recording into a month of posts
Take one workshop or podcast recording. Ask for the twelve ideas inside it, then for each one a short post in your tone. Schedule them. Content stops being a second job.
3
Your own replies into an enquiry helper
Paste your last twenty email replies to new enquiries. Ask the assistant to draft answers to the next ones using only those. You stay the voice; it saves the typing.
Start with whichever one annoys you most. One is enough.
